    About & Privacy

    NEORORA helps reviewers notice changes in posts over time.

    Screening tool, not a diagnosis.
    Protecting patient identity

    Before analysis, personal names in the research dataset were removed and replaced with coded patient IDs on a local machine. Identifiable data was not uploaded to Google Drive or other cloud storage during this preparation, helping protect patient privacy.

    • Use only posts you have permission to share.
    • Patient records use coded identifiers instead of names.
    • Automated masking may miss identifying information. Review files before sharing.

    Uploads are processed in the current session and are not saved to a database. Account-based access controls are not available in this prototype. Results support review by a clinician and do not provide a diagnosis.
